Geneva, 12 March 2024

Linked Data Summit Draws Record Attendance

By Maria Kovacs, World Tech News

The annual Linked Data Summit, hosted by the World Wide Web Consortium, attracted over two thousand delegates from more than sixty countries this year.

Key Announcements

Delegates heard presentations on advances in knowledge graph technologies and the growing adoption of JSON-LD across publishing, healthcare, and government sectors.
